# Your notes can read themselves

Press play and this note speaks in a natural voice while every word lights
up in sync, right inside the editor you already write in.

Click any word to jump the reading there, exactly like your favorite
reading app, and keep **editing while it reads**. The highlight never
touches your markdown syntax.

It works out of the box with a free voice, no account and no key, and it
remembers where you left off in every note. Reading speed, voices, and
providers are one click away on the floating pill below.

Your notes stay plain markdown files. They just learned to talk.
