## Security and Data Loss

Speaking Editor stores premium provider API keys in the Obsidian app's `localStorage` on the device where you enter them, and stores generated audio chunks in a per-device cache directory outside your vault. These mechanisms keep keys out of your synced vault files and keep audio off your synced notes, but they do not make all use risk-free. Keys in `localStorage` are not encrypted by an operating system keychain.

You are responsible for your local device security, backups, Obsidian vault, installed plugins, generated audio cache, and any notes you choose to read.
